# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in and around Listowel who want to see the menu, find the truck, check current hours, and call or text before visiting. Group and event organizers are a secondary audience for catering enquiries.

## Product Purpose

Help a hungry visitor decide what to order and get to BIGG DOGG with the least friction. The site is a public-facing concept for a local food truck and should make the menu, ordering phone number, location, hours, and real food photography easy to find on a phone.

## Positioning

BIGG DOGG is a Listowel street-food truck built around the original smash burger, freshly battered haddock, loaded fries, poutine, hot dogs, and generous portions made to order.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a phone while deciding where to eat or while travelling to the truck. The current concept points visitors to the Canadian Tire parking lot at 500 Mitchell Road South, Listowel, Ontario, and uses call or text ordering at 647-679-5006.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are served as assets by a Cloudflare Worker; editable source files are at the repository root and `dist/` is the published asset directory.
- The site includes a menu, location, hours, ordering links, catering contact, reviews, structured data, SEO metadata, security headers, and machine-readable context.
- Facebook updates are paused until the owner supplies valid Page access. Never expose or commit a Page access token.
- This is an unofficial website concept. Current menu prices, hours, phone number, location, social links, and image permissions require owner confirmation before official publication.
- Food photography must come from supplied or approved real images. Temporary stock images are explicitly marked as placeholders and must not be presented as BIGG DOGG food.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the BIGG DOGG name, supplied logo, truck photography, food photography, and the bold street-food character established by the truck.
- Use the truck and logo as the visual reference for the dark blue, red, yellow, warm paper, and flame-orange palette.
- Keep copy direct, local, generous, and useful. Do not invent testimonials, menu claims, prices, or business facts.

## Evidence on Hand

- Repository copy and menu content in `index.html`, `llms.txt`, and `README.md`.
- Supplied and approved-looking assets in `images/`, including the truck, logo, loaded fries, poutine, hot dog, kitchen, and menu graphic.
- A live concept at `https://bigg-dogg-listowel.mrbraddavidson.workers.dev/`.
- A customer review quote and Google rating currently displayed in the concept; confirm attribution and current rating before official publication.

## Product Principles

1. Put the food, menu, and ordering path first.
2. Make the local visit details findable without hunting.
3. Use real evidence and label placeholders honestly.
4. Keep the experience fast, readable, and thumb-friendly.
5. Preserve the truck's character while making every interaction clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and touch users, visible focus, readable contrast, descriptive image text, reduced-motion preferences, and layouts that remain usable at narrow phone widths and increased text sizes.

## Open Decisions

- Confirm the official menu, prices, hours, phone number, location, social links, catering details, review attribution, and image permissions with the owner.
- Replace the marked stock placeholders when the owner supplies approved BIGG DOGG photos.
