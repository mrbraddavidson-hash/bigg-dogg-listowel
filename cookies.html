BIGG DOGG keeps the site simple. This page describes the one first-party cookie currently used and the choices available to you.

The site may set bigg_dogg_cookie_notice after you select “Got it” in the cookie notice. It stores a value that tells the site the notice has been seen. It is a necessary preference cookie, set for up to one year, and does not contain your name, phone number, or order information.

No optional analytics, advertising, or social tracking cookies are currently loaded by this website. If that changes, this page and the cookie notice will be updated before optional tracking is introduced.

When you follow a link to Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram, or another external service, that service may use its own cookies or similar technologies. BIGG DOGG does not control those technologies. Consult the external service’s own privacy and cookie information.

4. Manage your choice

The “Cookie settings” link in the footer reopens the notice. You can also clear or block cookies through your browser controls. If you clear the preference cookie, the notice will appear again on a later visit.
