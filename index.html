Quick answers

BIGG DOGG
answers.

Planning your stop, a group order or a trip through Listowel? Here are the details people ask for most.

Where is the food truck in Listowel?

The truck parks in the Canadian Tire parking lot at 500 Mitchell Road South, Listowel, Ontario N4W 0C8. Get directions.

When is the food truck open?

The truck is open Monday through Saturday from 11 AM to 7 PM and closed Sunday. Hours can change, so check the Facebook page or call before you head out.

What is on the menu?

The menu includes the Original Smash Burger, freshly battered haddock fish and chips, hot dogs, chilli dogs, sausages, loaded fries, loaded nachos, poutine, chicken tenders, battered mushrooms and onion rings.

How do I order?

Call 647-679-5006 to order ahead. Combos come with fries and a drink, and any favourite can be made a combo for $5.00. Prices are plus tax and may change.

Does the food truck cater?

Yes. The team can discuss work lunches, tournaments, parties and community events. Text about catering with your date, headcount and location.
